What is Smart Coordinates?

Smart Coordinates is a neighbourhood intelligence tool. You enter where you live, or where you are thinking of moving, and we show how that neighbourhood has fared on safety, based on recorded FIR data from the Maharashtra Police.

What you get

1

A clear safety score

One number out of 100 that weighs how serious and how recent each incident was.

2

The full incident record

See every recorded incident with its date, place and type on a simple timeline.

3

Locality-level search

Search a neighbourhood or a street such as FC Road or Meethanagar.
